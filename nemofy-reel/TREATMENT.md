# Director's Treatment — "40GB For an AND Gate" (Nemofy StudentHub Instagram Reel)

## 1. Logline & Core Concept
Minutes before an 11:59 PM engineering lab deadline, 20-year-old CS/EE student **Leo** melts down over a broken 40GB desktop Verilog installer while his sharp-tongued digital AI companion **Nemo** roasts him—and resolves the whole crisis in 12 seconds using **StudentHub** on **https://nemofy.tech/** (100% free, zero-install browser Verilog simulator & waveforms, Python/C++/Java runner, binary calculators, and Word-to-PDF converter).

## 2. Characters (Existing Assets Preserved)
- **Leo (`Leo_Rig`)**: Blocky chibi 20-year-old college developer (`#3B5BDB` cobalt hoodie with twin white vertical chest stripes, `#1E1B4B` 5-spike hair, wide peach face, white blocky sneakers). Voice: smooth, youthful, expressive conversational male (`leo.onnx` / VoiceStudio).
- **Nemo (`Nemo_Rig`)**: Wide coral-terracotta 3D cube AI companion (`#D96043` / `#EA7B5F` warm side bevel, dark vertical pixel eyes, articulated mouth cavity + tongue, stubby waving arms, 4 stubby legs). Voice: smooth, witty, playfully sarcastic female (`nemo.onnx` / VoiceStudio).

## 3. Cinematography & Lookdev (Blender Authoritative Pipeline)
- **Format**: 9:16 Vertical (`1080×1920`, `30 FPS`, ~36s H.264 + AAC).
- **Lighting & Set**: Signature midnight rooftop/window-ledge studio overlooking glowing golden skyline towers and bokeh streetlights, anchored by a centerpiece **Nemofy StudentHub Holographic Console** (`Env_NemofyHoloScreen`) that rises dynamically at Beat 7 showing 3D illuminated **Verilog logic gates** and **live signal waveform bars**.
- **Camera Choreography**: 13 deliberate camera cuts (never > 3.9s on one beat) combining `crash-zoom` opening hook on Leo, tight `close-up` reaction cuts, `dolly-in` reveal on the Verilog waveform console, and a rhythmic `orbit` + `establishing-wide` pull-back loop into Beat 1.
- **Safe-Zone Typography**: On-screen hook card (`"3 HOURS WASTED INSTALLING"`) sits at Frame 1 inside Instagram's safe box (`y = 260`), and crisp burned-in dialogue captions sit cleanly at `y = 1230..1360` (`y < 1440`, zero overlap with characters' faces or IG UI).
